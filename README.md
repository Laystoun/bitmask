# BitMask

**bitmask** is a CLI application designed to empower users to protect themselves and their digital identities against the most common modern cyber threats. 

The application takes a multi-layered approach to security, mitigating risks that span from physical proximity attacks to digital tracking.

### Key Features & Defense Layers

*   **Physical & Network Proximity Defense:**
    *   **Wi-Fi Network Intrusion:** Prevents unauthorized access and alerts you to rogue devices breaking into your local network.
    *   **Traffic Interception:** Detects man-in-the-middle (MITM) attacks, packet sniffing, and malicious traffic analysis.
    *   **Device Snatching Protection:** Features anti-theft mechanisms (like local tripwires or automated lockdown triggers) if your laptop or computer is physically seized while active.

*   **Digital Identity & Privacy Protection:**
    *   **Browser & Device Fingerprinting Mitigation:** Blocks advanced tracking techniques that uniquely identify your hardware and OS profile.
    *   **Metadata Leak Prevention:** Strips identifying hidden markers from your network traffic and files before they leave your machine.
    *   **Privacy & Leak Auditing:** Monitors active connections to detect hidden telemetry, background tracking scripts, and unauthorized data outbound leaks.
